About Solyne

Solyne is a photo editor that applies an adjustable digital tan to selected skin areas. This policy describes the local photo-editing version of Solyne. It does not authorize cloud photo processing.

Photos and editing data

When you choose or take a photo, Solyne prepares a local JPEG copy, with its longest side limited to 2048 pixels. The editor uses machine-learning models to identify skin and facial landmarks and renders the adjustment locally. The active editor code does not send your photo, skin mask, facial landmarks or edited image to an image-generation service. Landmark detection supports editing; it is not used to identify you or create a face-recognition profile.

Working photo copies and exported previews can be held in the app's temporary/cache storage. Saved edits contain a local before/after pair and metadata such as creation time, dimensions and selected preset. Saving to Photos creates a separate copy in your system photo library. Solyne does not offer an account or its own cloud photo library in this version.

Network requests and other services

The current editor downloads MediaPipe runtime files and models from jsDelivr and Google-hosted storage. Those services receive ordinary network connection information, including an IP address and request information, under their own policies. These downloads do not require sending your selected photo. First use requires internet access. Your system photo provider may separately retrieve an image from services such as iCloud according to your settings.

Opening our website or Apple links makes a connection to those websites. Website hosting, access logs and any additional website processing must be described in the published website notice before that site launches. The app currently has no integrated advertising, behavioral analytics or third-party crash-reporting SDK. We do not use your photos for advertising, model training or sale.

Permissions

Photo selection uses the system picker to access the image you choose. Camera access is requested when you choose to take a photo. Saving an edit requests permission to add it to Photos. You can manage permissions in device Settings. Refusing a permission may prevent its related action; it does not authorize other data use.

Purchases

Purchases are not enabled in the current preview build. If subscriptions launch, Apple will process payment under its own terms. Before enabling purchases, we will update this policy to identify any subscription service, transaction identifiers, entitlement records, retention and recipients actually used. Do not send us payment-card information.

Retention and deletion

Saved edit pairs remain in the app's storage until removed. Deleting a saved edit removes that pair and its metadata from the app; it does not delete your original in Photos or copies you exported or shared. Clearing a selected photo clears the current session reference; it does not promise immediate erasure of temporary files. Cache files may remain until the operating system clears them or app data is removed.

Removing the app normally removes its local app data. Offloading an app can preserve data. Device/cloud backups and Photos copies follow your device and provider settings and may persist separately. Solyne cannot remotely erase local files on your phone; use the app's delete controls and device storage/backup settings.

Children and other people's photos

Solyne is not directed to children under 13. If local law requires a higher age or parental permission, those rules apply. Use only photos you have the right and appropriate permission to edit, particularly photos of children. Contact us if you believe a child has sent personal information to us. This statement is not an age-verification mechanism or a claim of Kids Category eligibility.
