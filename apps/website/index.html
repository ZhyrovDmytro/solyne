Good to know

A few little details.

Can I download Solyne yet?

Not yet. Solyne is in development for iPhone. There is no public App Store release or active purchase available at this time.

Does it change my real skin?

No. Solyne applies a cosmetic effect to a photograph. It is not tanning advice, skin protection or a prediction of how your skin will respond to the sun.

How are my photos processed?

The current editor applies the effect locally. It downloads editing models and runtime files from external hosts, so first use needs internet. See the Privacy Policy draft for current data handling and limitations.

Will I need a subscription?

Editing and saving are planned as Solyne Plus features. Final plans and localized prices will be shown before any purchase. Purchases are not enabled in the current build.

Where can I get help?

Visit Solyne Support or email support@nummio.fyi and mention Solyne.
