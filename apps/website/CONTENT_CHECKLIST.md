# Landing-page content and design handoff

## Design contract
The page should explain the adjustable photo effect, let visitors inspect it, demonstrate the editing workflow, establish trust through real evidence, clarify Plus benefits, then invite a concrete next step. Keep Solyne's cream/olive/editorial style and direct-photo comparison. Mobile sections stack, touch targets remain usable and keyboard comparison remains supported. Never substitute fabricated proof for missing content.

Current CTA: email to ask about beta access. This is an inquiry, not guaranteed beta enrollment or a mailing-list subscription. When the App Store page is live, replace the final email CTA with the official download badge and verified product URL, and update the hero/navigation CTA to point to that section. Do not claim download availability prematurely.

## Replace the visible TO ADD blocks
- **Examples 01–03:** three real before/after pairs exported by the app: daylight portrait; arms/shoulders and clothing boundaries; different skin tone and lighting. Record intensity/settings, identical crops, subject permission and image rights. Do not use the illustrative hero as proof of actual output. Add useful alt text.
- **Walkthrough:** 15–25 seconds of actual app use, from selection through intensity/refinement/comparison/save. Supply a poster image, controls and captions or a text equivalent. No autoplay sound. Use a still screenshot if video is not ready.
- **Verified feedback:** 2–3 authentic, permissioned user quotes with the user's chosen attribution, beta/release context and disclosure of incentives. No invented stars, counts, logos or quotes. Remove the whole section if real feedback is unavailable.
- **Offer details:** confirmed products, features, billing periods, localized price strategy and trial/renewal terms. Match the actual store offer. Do not copy illustrative design prices into the website as live prices.
- **App Store CTA:** verified listing URL; official Apple-provided badge; supported OS version from the final build. Optional desktop QR code should encode the same verified URL. Pre-order badge only if pre-orders are enabled.

## Before public launch
Fill legal operator/address and other outstanding policy fields; remove draft notices only after review. Configure hosting and publish all legal routes. Confirm rights to all example imagery, including existing artwork. Remove every editorial placeholder or replace it with reviewed content. Update “coming soon” statements consistently when released. If adding analytics, forms or a waitlist, implement actual data handling and update privacy information before collecting data.

## Validation for this revision
Lint passed. Browser inspection at 320, 768, 1024 and 1440 CSS pixels found no horizontal overflow; all images loaded. The final primary CTA targets the launch section. Pointer/keyboard comparison retained. This is not a full accessibility audit or physical-device test.
