Editing and saving

First-time model loading needs an internet connection. Retry loading if it fails. Use Add/Remove and Undo to refine the selected skin area. Exported PNGs are limited by the normalized source, currently up to 2048 pixels on the longest side. If saving fails, check available device storage and Solyne's Photos permission in Settings.

Delete an edit

Open My edits, open the saved pair and choose delete. This removes the app's saved pair, not copies in Photos, backups or other apps. Archive access is currently membership-gated; if you cannot reach it, contact support for device-storage guidance. You can remove the app and its data using iOS storage controls; offloading can preserve data. Remove exported copies separately in Photos if desired. Starting over clears the selected-photo session, not necessarily temporary files immediately.

Subscriptions

Purchases and restore are unavailable in the current preview. Once subscriptions launch, manage or cancel Apple purchases at https://apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions and request refunds at https://reportaproblem.apple.com/ . Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Privacy

Read the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use linked alongside this page. Solyne has no user accounts in the current version, so there is no Solyne account-deletion process. Ask privacy questions at support@nummio.fyi.
