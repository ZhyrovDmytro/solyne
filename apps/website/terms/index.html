The service

Solyne provides digital photo-editing tools for an adjustable tan effect. Results are cosmetic simulations, not a prediction of sun exposure, a medical assessment, skin protection or advice to tan. Results vary with the photo, selection and device. Review the result before saving or sharing. Exports currently use a normalized source of up to 2048 pixels on the longest side, rather than original camera resolution.
